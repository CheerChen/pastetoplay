Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-14

PasteToPlay is a VR video player for Meta Quest, developed by an individual as a personal project. This policy describes what the app does and does not do with data.

No accounts, no backend

The app has no user accounts and no server backend operated by the developer. You do not sign in, and the developer does not operate any service that the app connects to.

No analytics or advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. Unity Analytics, Unity Crash Reporting, and Unity Purchasing are disabled. No usage data, telemetry, or crash reports are sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The only data the app stores is a list of recently pasted video URLs and per-link resume positions, kept on the headset using Unity PlayerPrefs. This data is never transmitted to the developer or any third party. You can clear it in-app at any time using the Clear button next to the Recent links list, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network connections the app makes

  • Media URLs you provide. The app connects directly from your headset to the video URL you paste or enter. The developer never sees these URLs.
  • DLNA servers on your local network. When you use the DLNA browser, the app sends SSDP multicast and HTTP requests to DLNA media servers on your LAN that you select.
  • Local read-ahead proxy. The app runs a proxy on 127.0.0.1 (localhost) inside the device for buffered playback. This never leaves the headset.

Android permissions

  • INTERNET — to stream the video URLs you provide.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to obtain the multicast lock for DLNA SSDP discovery.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — to hold the WifiManager MulticastLock during DLNA discovery.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check network connectivity before playback.

No data collection

No data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties. The developer does not have access to your video URLs, your DLNA server contents, or your viewing activity.
