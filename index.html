PasteToPlay

Paste a link, it just plays — auto-detects 180/360, SBS/TB, fisheye.

Paste to play

Paste a video URL from the Quest clipboard into the address bar, or type one in. The app connects directly to the source — no companion app, no server, no account.

Auto format detection

View (flat / 180 / 360), Stereo (mono / SBS / TB), and Lens (rectilinear / equirect / fisheye) are read from the video frames at playback start. Each axis can be switched manually from the dock.

DLNA browsing

Browse and play from DLNA media servers on your local network.

Native playback

ExoPlayer decoding: MP4, HLS, DASH, SmoothStreaming. Automatic fallback to Unity VideoPlayer if the native path fails.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting. The only data stored is your recently pasted URL history, kept on-device and never transmitted.
